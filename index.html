Gary wants his flamingos back.

Gary says he bought those lawn flamingos. His neighbour disagrees. Turn one over, shine the Purple Light under its wing and decide whose story holds up.

Your bench, your ruling

Hear 75 cases across five courts. Weigh an exhibit, ask a question at the Lie Detector, or open the Case Book and match a fact to a claim. Stamp GUILTY or NOT GUILTY, then strike the gavel. A lawyer might offer you a shortcut. The Inspector might see you take it.

Off the clock

Read the Gazette in Chambers, spend your court earnings on a gavel or throw one at the moving cast in Gavel Knockout.

Single player on Quest 3 and Quest 3S. Play seated or standing, with hands or Touch controllers. English voices and text; no internet connection required for play and no in-app purchases.
