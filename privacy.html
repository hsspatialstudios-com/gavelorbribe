Gavel or Bribe privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy explains what information Gavel or Bribe (“the game”) handles and why. The game is made by HS Spatial Studios (“we”, “us”) for Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S and is sold on the Meta Horizon Store.

The short version

  • The game works offline. It has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information. The game cannot send anything to us: it does not request internet access, and it does not request camera, microphone or location permission.
  • You buy the game through Meta, and Meta processes the payment. We never see your payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

What the game stores on your headset

  • Progress and settings: your career and case results, in-game money, the gavels and outfits you own, and preferences such as text size, volumes and input mode.
  • Pictures the game draws itself: up to 12 “Gazette” press photos of your in-game judge, and any picture you take with the Snap 9:16 button after a ruling. They show the game’s virtual courtroom. The game never uses the headset’s cameras.

These files stay in the game’s own storage on your headset.

Head, hand and controller tracking

The headset gives the game the positions of your head, controllers and, if you play with hand tracking, your hands, so that you can see your hands, pick things up and play. They are used only on the headset while you play. Instant Replay keeps the last few seconds of them in memory to show a replay of a ruling, then discards them. Tracking data is never saved to storage or sent anywhere.

Cloud backup

If Cloud Backup is turned on for your Meta account, Meta may back up the game’s progress and settings so you can restore them after reinstalling or on a new headset. The pictures are not included. Meta handles this backup under Meta’s privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking.
  • No access to your cameras, microphone, location, room scan or contacts. The game does not use Meta’s Platform SDK, so it never sees your Meta user ID, name, friends list or payment details.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with anyone.

Children

The game is made for teens and adults (13 and over). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • On the headset: uninstall the game. Its progress, settings and pictures are deleted with it.
  • Cloud backup: on the headset, open Settings > General > Cloud backup > Manage backup, or sign in to secure.oculus.com/my/cloud-backup on a computer, and delete the game’s backup.
  • Purchases: Meta keeps your purchase history. For questions about a charge or a refund, contact Meta support.

Contact and privacy requests

Email hsspatialstudios@gmail.com with questions or requests. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you, do not sell or share them, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time. Apart from such emails, we hold no personal data about you. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Changes

If the game’s data handling changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.
