Gavel or Bribe privacy policy

Prepared 22 November 2026. This policy describes the store edition of Gavel or Bribe by HS Spatial Studios.

What stays on the headset

The game saves settings and career progress locally. The headset supplies head, controller and optional hand poses to position you and let you pick up and use things. The app does not request internet, camera, microphone or location permission and does not transmit these poses or your save to the studio. There is no in-game account or chat.

Store and operating system

The platform handles store transactions and may back up local app data according to your device and account settings. These services are separate from the game. Manage platform purchases and backups through your Meta account.

Removing your data

Use the headset's storage settings to clear Gavel or Bribe's app data. This removes local progress and preferences. If you use cloud backup, also manage or delete the game's backup through Meta's backup controls to prevent restoration.

Contact and privacy requests

If you email the studio, we receive your address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. For questions or to request deletion of a support message, email hsspatialstudios@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or payment details.
