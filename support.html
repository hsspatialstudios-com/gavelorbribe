At the bench

How do I reach a verdict?

Listen to the charge and the defendant. Use the tools available in this case. Open FILE in the Case Book to connect a fact to a claim. Choose the GUILTY or NOT GUILTY stamp, mark the book and strike the sounding block with the gavel to finish.

What if the needle says NERVOUS?

Compare the answer with the physical evidence. A nervous person can be innocent. Look for a fact that backs up or contradicts the story about the charge.
